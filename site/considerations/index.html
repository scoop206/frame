considerations

Ideas we've weighed.

Agent tooling moves fast, and plenty of good ideas don't fit every tool. These are ideas and technologies we've weighed against frame's design, and where we landed on each: what we'd take, what we'd skip, and what would change our minds.

  • doneA2A: the Agent2Agent protocolAn open standard for agents to find and message each other. It maps onto frame's broker almost one to one. We weighed it, didn't adopt the protocol, and shipped its task model into the broker.
